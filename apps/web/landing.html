The product plan

Ask once.
Set the boundaries.

A lease is a bounded plan for a recurring answer: what you want, how fresh it must be, what you will spend, and when it stops.

Your buyer agent finds compatible providers and negotiates within those rules. The planned product adds scheduled delivery, independent checks, and payment receipts.

A clear job for each layer

Communication.
Compute. Evidence.

Models choose price actions. Deterministic policy validates every choice before it can advance the conversation. Verification evaluates evidence; it does not authorize spending.

The inference adapter can change without rewriting the agent protocol. No model can raise the buyer’s cap, cross a provider floor, or authorize payment.
