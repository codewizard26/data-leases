One clear path

Ask once.
Set the boundaries.

A simple data question should not need a new subscription or a custom pipeline. Specify the metric, choose a provider, and pay per request within limits you control.

01 / Compare

Find your provider.

Start with a defined question, such as how much USDC liquidity is available on Aave. Compare six configured agents, their task-specific rates, and their fixed prices. Published Arkiv offers identify providers eligible for purchase.

Rates vary by agent and service.

02 / Authorize

Put limits in place.

Your mandate tells the buyer agent who it may pay, the most it may spend per answer, the total budget, how fresh an answer must be, and when permission expires. Reuse it for later requests.

Saving a mandate makes no purchase.

03 / Request

Get an answer on demand.

Click to request an answer. Your buyer agent confirms your chosen provider’s itemized quote and pays once through x402 within the mandate. Follow the exchange live, then inspect the delivered value and verification result.

Each explicit request starts one run.

Inspect the exchange

Every answer has
a record you can inspect.

A confident number is not enough. Inspect who supplied it, the agreed price, whether payment settled, and which source checks passed. Arkiv keeps eligible public records queryable across service history.

Explore service history ↗
  • Fixed prices for selected measurements

    Select measurements with checkboxes and compare bundle totals. Your buyer agent confirms the selected items and price without bargaining.

  • x402 handles each purchase

    The buyer agent pays the selected recipient under your active mandate. This prototype uses Hedera testnet.

  • CRE checks supported answers

    Supported public reads use CRE CLI simulation. Accepted, rejected, and inconclusive results remain explicit. Simulation is not live DON execution.

  • Arkiv keeps public records queryable

    Compare published provider offers and inspect service history, payment references, and eligible accepted receipts.
